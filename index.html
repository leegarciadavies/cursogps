In the course you’ll learn

3 simple steps

When the dot is on the line, you’re on the right path.

We show you what to do when it isn’t – without panic, and without touching 20 buttons.

In the city, Google Maps decides for you. In the mountains, you’re on your own.

Most new hikers come from road navigation: they enter a destination and simply obey the voice. But on a trail, maps are less documented, tracks can be wrong, and apps sometimes “recalculate” in dangerous ways.

If you’re over 50 or just not very techy, this can feel overwhelming: different apps, strange words like track and waypoint, and devices with menus that look like a cockpit.

Follow the Line focuses on one simple goal: you + a line on the screen + the confidence to know what to do if something goes wrong.

Typical frustrations we target

  • ! “The GPS shows the track upside down. I don’t know which direction to go when I rejoin the line.”
  • ! “Komoot or other apps change the route and send me somewhere dangerous or far from the safe path.”
  • ! “People say ‘easy hike’ but the track passes next to a cliff. I don’t know how to read the track before going.”
  • ! “I had to click 20 times to send one route to my Garmin. I just want something simple.”

This course doesn’t try to turn you into a cartography expert. It simply teaches you the minimum you need to stop feeling lost with your device.

How “Follow the Line” works

Prototype · We’re shaping it with real feedback
1

Short, visual lessons

Three short video modules that you can watch on your phone: load track, follow the line, and what to do if you drift away. No more than 15–20 minutes in total.

2

Printable “cheat sheet”

A one-page PDF you can print or save on your phone, with a simple 3-step protocol: “If I’m not on the line, I do this, then this, then this”. Easy to use on the trail, even when you’re nervous.

3

Practice track + feedback

You’ll receive a simple practice track to test near home. After that, you can answer a short survey to tell us if it actually helped you feel safer and what you’d pay for the full version.

FAQ – Before you sign up

Do I need advanced technical skills?

No. The course is specifically designed for people who feel uncomfortable with technology. If you can open your GPS app and press “play” on a video, you’re good.

Does it cover maps, waypoints and advanced stuff?

Only very lightly, and only when it helps you follow the line. Our promise: no unnecessary theory, no deep cartography. Just what helps you not get lost.

Will it work with my GPS or app?

We use examples with popular apps (like Komoot or Wikiloc) and with typical outdoor GPS devices. The logic – load track, follow line, return to line – is the same in nearly all systems.
